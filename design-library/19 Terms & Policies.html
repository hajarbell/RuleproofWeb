Information Security

Your compliance data deserves more than a promise.

RuleProof is designed for organisations that work with policies, controls, training records and audit evidence. Security therefore has to be part of how the platform is built and operated, not a paragraph added after the fact.

Our security approach is risk-based and combines technical, organisational and operational safeguards intended to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information entrusted to RuleProof.

We limit access to systems and customer information to authorised people and services that require access for legitimate operational purposes. Access is managed according to role and responsibility, reviewed as appropriate, and removed when no longer required. Administrative access is subject to stronger controls than ordinary user access.

RuleProof uses authentication and session controls designed to reduce unauthorised access. Customers are expected to protect their credentials and use available security features appropriately. We may introduce or require additional authentication controls for sensitive functions or higher-risk environments.

RuleProof uses industry-standard secure transport mechanisms to protect information transmitted between users and the Services. Stored information is protected using encryption and other infrastructure safeguards appropriate to the nature and risk of the data.

Security is considered throughout product development and change management. We use development, review, testing and deployment practices designed to reduce the risk of introducing vulnerabilities or unauthorised changes. Access to production environments and sensitive configuration is restricted.

We maintain processes to identify, assess, prioritise and remediate security vulnerabilities. The approach may include automated scanning, dependency monitoring, security review and independent testing appropriate to the maturity and risk profile of the Services.

We maintain logging and monitoring designed to support security operations, troubleshooting and investigation of suspicious activity. We maintain incident-response procedures for assessing, containing, remediating and learning from security events. Where an incident triggers a legal or contractual notification obligation, we notify affected customers or authorities as required.

We use cloud-based infrastructure and operational safeguards designed to support service resilience. Backup, recovery and continuity measures are maintained according to the needs and risk profile of the Services. We review these measures as the platform evolves.

Personnel with access to confidential or customer information are subject to confidentiality obligations and access restrictions appropriate to their responsibilities. Security responsibilities are communicated internally and access is adjusted when roles change or access is no longer required.

RuleProof relies on selected technology and service providers to operate parts of the Services. We assess providers in a manner proportionate to the service and risk, use contractual protections where appropriate and limit provider access to what is necessary. Providers that process personal data on behalf of RuleProof are subject to applicable data protection requirements.

Security is shared. Customers are responsible for managing their users and permissions, protecting credentials, configuring the Services appropriately, maintaining secure endpoint devices, ensuring Customer Content is lawfully uploaded and promptly reporting suspected compromise or misuse.

RuleProof’s security programme is informed by recognised information-security and data-protection principles. We do not present a certification, audit opinion or formal compliance status as achieved unless it has actually been completed and is current. Customers conducting security due diligence may request available assurance information through the RuleProof team, subject to confidentiality and security considerations.

If you believe you have identified a security vulnerability affecting RuleProof, please contact us through the website and clearly mark the communication as a security report. Do not access, alter, retain or disclose customer data, disrupt the Services, use destructive testing or publicly disclose a suspected vulnerability before RuleProof has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate it.
