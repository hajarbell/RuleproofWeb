Strong compliance starts with clear obligations

Turn regulations, standards, guidelines and internal policies into actionable obligations shaped by how your business actually operates, ready for your team to review and approve in minutes.

Trusted by leading companies

Implement regulatory changes in weeks, not months

Every regulatory change can trigger the same manual cycle: read the documents, cross-check supporting requirements, decide what's relevant and determine what needs to change across policies and controls.

Proof connects the process, starting with clear obligations your team can review and carry forward.

Make every obligation specific to your business

A regulatory requirement doesn't exist in isolation from the business it applies to.

Proof analyses regulations alongside standards, guidelines, internal policies and business context such as your operations and company information, so the obligations it surfaces reflect how your organisation actually works.

Catch what connects across documents

Proof follows relationships between clauses and paragraphs, identifying complementary or conflicting requirements and bringing them together where needed.

This reduces duplication before it makes its way into your policies and controls.

See exactly where every obligation came from

Trace an obligation back to the document, article, paragraph and supporting context it came from.

Your team can verify the interpretation for themselves instead of relying on an AI output they can't explain.

Keep every decision on record

Approvals, amendments, rejections and requirements marked as non-relevant are recorded as they happen, creating an audit trail from the first interpretation.

From regulatory documents to approved obligations in minutes

01

Bring in the full context

Upload regulatory documents alongside relevant standards, guidelines, internal policies and information about your business.

02

Extract and structure obligations

Proof AI analyses the documents together, follows relationships between clauses and paragraphs and structures obligations using the context you've provided.

03

Review and approve

Every obligation is surfaced for your team to review alongside its exact sources and supporting context.

Edit, approve or reject it while keeping full control over what moves forward.

04

Move into policy mapping

Once approved, obligations move into Policy Mapping, where you can map them against existing policies, identify gaps and decide what needs to be amended or created.
